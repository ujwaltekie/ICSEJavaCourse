Why waiting for Class X can cost students

  • Class X moves on to arrays, strings, constructors and inheritance, and all of them assume loops, conditions and objects are already second nature.
  • Students who start in Class IX meet each Class X topic as a natural next step, not a brand-new mountain.
  • Starting early leaves time for practice, revision and doubt-clearing before the boards.

  • Classes are conducted in small batches of 2–3 students.
  • Conducted online on Zoom for 50 to 60 minutes.

Student dashboard

Every student gets a dashboard with a complete record of class dates, topics covered and assignments provided, so progress can be tracked anytime.

Rewards & community

Access to the Student Discord Community for coding discussions and events. Students earn points for participation and performance, redeemable in the Rewards Store. Details are shared on Day 1.

Class policy

If a student misses 2 classes without prior notice, 1 class will be deducted. Informing us 1 day in advance keeps sessions smooth and fair for everyone.

Leave & rescheduling

Need to skip a class for any reason or event? Please inform us at least 1 day in advance.
